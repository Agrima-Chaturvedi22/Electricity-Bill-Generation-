Problem Statement-In years household electricity consumption has increased significantly due to the rapid proliferation of electrical appliances. However individual consumers often struggle to monitor, predict and control their utility expenses. Traditional billing models rely on tiered "slab" tariff structures where the cost per unit increases as total consumption crosses specific thresholds alongside additional fixed service charges and taxes. Because monthly energy bills are delivered after the consumption cycle ends users lack real-time visibility into how specific appliances contribute to their costs. Consequently consumers face financial burdens and fail to identify energy-inefficient habits.
To address this challenge there is a need, for an automated modular Electricity Bill Estimator & Consumption Analyzer. The proposed system enables users to register household appliances with their wattage ratings and daily operational hours. By converting device parameters into monthly kilowatt-hour metrics and applying customizable slab-based pricing algorithms the application generates real-time accurate billing estimates. This project empowers consumers to analyze appliance-level energy breakdowns, forecast expenses and adopt proactive energy-saving practices.
